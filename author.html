Author

Akhil Ranjan

Investigative journalist focused on misinformation, digital manipulation, evidence verification, and public accountability.

Akhil Ranjan reports across borders and languages, with a focus on how digital evidence is preserved, interpreted, and weaponized in modern public life.

His work blends field reporting, open-source intelligence, and editorial rigor into stories that help audiences understand both the mechanism and the impact of information disorder.
