How can investigative journalists hold political actors accountable in an era of rising autocracy, political violence, AI-driven disinformation, and other anti-democratic threats in this seismic global elections period? ICFJ Disarming Disinformation Project (2024)

I was one of eight investigative journalists selected from around the world for ICFJ's prestigious 2024 Disarming Disinformation project, focused on reporting on organised abuse of information and information technologies aimed at undermining elections and democratic processes. As part of the project, I investigated coordinated digital disinformation campaigns during India's 2024 general elections and led the production of an in-depth video documentary exposing a network of YouTubers amplifying Hindu nationalist narratives through staged "vox pop"–style videos.
